# China's Education System and Policies in the AI Era, with International Comparisons (current to late Nov 2026)

Method note: Research used web search with summarized results. Direct fetches of several Chinese government and media hosts (nda.gov.cn, eol.cn) failed in this session's network, so many findings come from search-result summaries of official pages and major media rather than a full reading of the original text. Exact clause wording should be checked against the originals before quoting verbatim in the book. Status labels: [IMPLEMENTED] = in force or taking place; [ANNOUNCED] = issued as a plan or target, implementation pending or unverified.

## 1. Chinese AI education policy (K-12, gaokao, national strategy, 2026 updates)

### Takeaway
China has moved quickly from guidance to a multi-layered mandate. There are two MOE-committee guidelines (May 2025), Beijing's minimum of 8 class hours per year (since autumn 2025), a five-ministry "AI+Education" Action Plan (April 2026), and the State Council's 15th Five-Year Education Plan (June 2026), which calls for AI education across all school stages. The approach is staged by age: primary pupils may not use open-ended generative AI on their own, and "代劳式" use (letting AI do the work) is banned at every level. During the gaokao, AI chatbots switch off photo recognition of exam questions.

### Cited Findings
**National guidelines (May 2025) [IMPLEMENTED as guidance]**
- 教育部基础教育教学指导委员会 issued 《中小学人工智能通识教育指南（2025年版）》 and 《中小学生成式人工智能使用指南（2025年版）》 together in mid-May 2025. Xinhua reported it on 2025-05-13, and gov.cn reprinted the People's Daily report on 2025-05-15. — [新华网](http://www.news.cn/tech/20250513/4ef8cb3c90b44cb28124297a44aeb455/c.html); [gov.cn](https://app.www.gov.cn/govdata/gov/202505/15/528972/article.html); [浙江省教育考试院](https://www.zjzs.net/art/2025/5/12/art_320_11161.html)
- The 通识教育指南 sets out a "分层递进、螺旋上升" (layered, spiraling) general AI education system. It builds a "四位一体" AI literacy that combines knowledge, skills, thinking and values, and gives implementation advice on classroom teaching, organisation, evaluation and teaching research. — [北京教科院 full text](https://www.bjesr.cn/ywbm/jyfzyjzx/zyxz/2025-05-27/53910.html); [成都 reprint](http://www.cdfirstcityedu.com/news-detail.aspx?t=33&ContentId=1554)
- The 生成式AI使用指南 sets rules by school stage:
  - **Primary:** pupils are prohibited from using open-ended content-generation functions on their own (禁止学生独自使用开放式内容生成功能). They should use AI with help from teachers and parents.
  - **Junior secondary:** students may moderately explore the logic of generated content and are taught to cross-check it.
  - **Senior secondary:** students may do inquiry learning that draws on how the technology works, and assess AI's social impact.
  - **Students:** may not submit AI output directly as homework or exam answers ("杜绝'代劳'").
  - **Teachers:** may not use AI as a substitute teaching subject, may not grade students directly with AI-generated content, and may not answer student questions directly with AI.
  — [澎湃](https://www.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_30802716); [中新网 "杜绝'代劳'"](https://www.chinanews.com.cn/cj/2025/05-12/10414191.shtml); [eol.cn policy reprint](https://www.eol.cn/zhengce/wenjian/202505/t20250512_2667831.shtml)
- An expert interviewed by Xinhua said the guideline is directional and should not be applied as a blanket ban. Self-disciplined students could be given more latitude. — [新华网 talking](https://www.news.cn/talking/20250522/d6bed8f9186c4442b64c2b7861aafff5/c.html)

**Beijing's mandatory class hours [IMPLEMENTED from autumn 2025]**
- In March 2025 the 北京市教委 issued 《北京市推进中小学人工智能教育工作方案（2025—2027年）》.
  - From the autumn 2025 semester, all primary and secondary schools in Beijing must offer general AI education of **at least 8 class hours per school year**.
  - Schools may run a standalone course or fold it into 信息科技, 通用技术, 科学, 综合实践 or 劳动.
  - Each stage has a different emphasis: experiential in primary, cognitive in junior secondary, integrative and practice-based in senior secondary.
  — [新京报](https://www.bjnews.com.cn/detail/1741356871129813.html); [澎湃](https://m.thepaper.cn/detail/30333531); [IT之家](https://www.ithome.com/0/879/519.htm)
- Beijing also published 《北京市中小学人工智能教育地方课程纲要（试行）（2025年版）》, which reportedly requires AI course results to be included in students' 综合素质评价 (comprehensive quality evaluation). — [新京报](https://www.bjnews.com.cn/detail/1741873684168067.html); [cls.cn](https://www.cls.cn/detail/2070852)

**教育强国建设规划纲要（2024—2035年） [IMPLEMENTED as strategy]**
- Issued by 中共中央、国务院 and reported by Xinhua on 2025-01-19. — [光明网/新华社](https://m.gmw.cn/toutiao/2025-01/19/content_37806225.htm); [gov.cn](https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/202501/content_6999914.htm)
- It requires "以教育数字化开辟发展新赛道、塑造发展新优势" (using education digitalization to open new tracks and build new advantages). An 经济日报 commentary reads it as making AI-driven education reform a key element, including building curricula and textbooks suited to the AI era. — [新华网](https://www.news.cn/tech/20250123/27ccdcd1c5bf431c8b4ea817180b6a36/c.html)
- Gap: the exact AI clauses of the 纲要 were not retrieved (see Gaps).

**"人工智能+教育"行动计划 (2026) [ANNOUNCED; implementation under way]**
- Five ministries led by MOE issued 《"人工智能+教育"行动计划》 (教科信〔2026〕1号). It was dated 2026-04-02 and published 2026-04-10. It cites the 教育强国纲要 as its basis. — [教育部教育管理信息中心](https://www.emic.edu.cn/site/webfile/zcwj/2026-04-10/7755.html); [国家数据局](https://www.nda.gov.cn/sjj/zwgk/zcfb/0413/20260410162428109058250_pc.html); [gov.cn policy library](https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202604/content_7065138.htm)
- K-12 provisions:
  - Keep improving the 通识教育指南 and ensure AI courses are "开齐、开足、开好" (offered in full, with enough hours, and taught well).
  - Bring AI into local curricula.
  - Encourage interdisciplinary teaching and use in after-school services (课后服务).
  - Bring AI into teacher-qualification exams and certification.
  — [emic](https://www.emic.edu.cn/site/webfile/zcwj/2026-04-10/7755.html); [smartcity policy digest](https://www.smartcity.team/policies/%E5%A4%A7%E6%A8%A1%E5%9E%8B%E6%94%BF%E7%AD%96%E5%BA%93/%E4%BA%BA%E5%B7%A5%E6%99%BA%E8%83%BD%E6%95%99%E8%82%B2%E8%A1%8C%E5%8A%A8%E8%AE%A1%E5%88%92/)
- MOE goal: by **2030**, a development pattern of deep AI–education integration should be "basically formed" (基本形成). — [moe.gov.cn, 2026-04-14](http://www.moe.gov.cn/fbh/live/2026/77927/mtbd/202604/t20260414_1433545.html)
- Vocational education: the plan calls for upgrading traditional majors and a cluster-training programme for high-skilled AI talent. — [新华网 via search digest](https://www.news.cn/politics/20260716/8e1eb894423d49e2ba4f370210b0576c/c.html)

**"三进" (February 2026) [ANNOUNCED]**
- The national basic-education work meeting in February 2026 reportedly called for AI to enter curriculum standards, daily teaching, and exams and evaluation (进课程标准、进日常教学、进考试评价). This comes from a secondary source (Zhihu column). — [知乎](https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/2013278729510605667)
- Claims that AI literacy will enter 综合素质评价 nationwide also come from secondary commentary (Toutiao) and are unverified at national level. — [今日头条](https://www.toutiao.com/article/7607631535998648872/)

**教育发展"十五五"规划 [ANNOUNCED, 2026–2030]**
- State Council document 国发〔2026〕19号, dated 2026-06-22 and reported by Xinhua on 2026-06-29. It says "推进人工智能全学段教育，提升学生人工智能素养，增强提出问题、解决问题的能力" (advance AI education across all school stages, raise students' AI literacy, and strengthen their ability to pose and solve problems). — [gov.cn 公报](https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2026/issue_12846/202607/content_7074813.html); [gov.cn](https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/content/202606/content_7073640.htm); [新华网](https://www.news.cn/politics/20260629/317b8a2594f34eb2a562d242033277e7/c.html); [东方财富](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202606293786741053.html)
- The State Council Information Office (国新办) held a press conference on 2026-09-23 on building 教育强国 during the 15th Five-Year period. — [新华网](https://www.news.cn/20260923/c314373f226b460884d49f0a858b1d15/c.html)

**AI and the gaokao [IMPLEMENTED, 2025 and 2026]**
- **2025 gaokao:** 通义千问 and 豆包 stopped responding to exam-paper images, while Kimi and 腾讯元宝 suspended image recognition entirely during exam hours. — [IT之家](https://www.ithome.com/0/859/550.htm)
- **2026 gaokao (June 2026):** media reported that 豆包, 通义千问, 元宝, Kimi, DeepSeek and 文心一言 would restrict functions during exam periods.
  - 豆包 customer service said 拍题答疑 (photo-a-question help) would be disabled.
  - Uploads of exam questions reportedly returned "内容不合规" (non-compliant content).
  - Platforms' statements were inconsistent, and no unified official list was published.
  — [凤凰科技](https://tech.ifeng.com/c/8tRDOb1IzJf); [36氪](https://www.36kr.com/p/3825861613687426); [东方财富](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202605273750945126.html)

### Inferences
- National guidance sets the direction, but mandatory hours are set by provinces and cities. Beijing's 8 hours a year is roughly one lesson a month, so school AI coverage is introductory literacy, not deep skill-building.
- Chinese policy for children under about 12 is close to UNESCO's caution: supervised use only. Parents who hand a primary-age child an open chatbot are going beyond school policy.
- The gaokao shutdown shows the state treats AI as a cheating threat in high-stakes testing. Core exam performance still depends on unaided skill.

### Gaps
- The exact AI text in the 教育强国纲要 was not retrieved. Search found only commentary. Verify at gov.cn/zhengce/202501/content_6999914.htm.
- Other provinces' mandatory hours (e.g., Shanghai, Zhejiang, Guangdong, Shandong) were not verified in this session.
- A 2024 MOE office notice on strengthening K-12 AI education (believed to be issued Dec 2024) could not be sourced in this session.
- No official confirmation was found that AI literacy is formally scored in 中考 or 高考.

## 2. 双减 status, tutoring market, AI learning devices (学习机), and effectiveness evidence

### Takeaway
Five years after 双减 (July 2021), the crackdown on academic-subject tutoring remains in place. The listed education giants have recovered revenue through non-academic tutoring, study abroad, adult education and hardware, especially AI learning devices. The 学习机 market grew fast in 2024–25 but is forecast to slow to about 5% growth in 2026. There is no independent evidence that these devices improve learning, and consumer complaints are common.

### Cited Findings
- **Industry contraction:** a report cited by Sina Finance (May 2026) says tutoring institutions fell from about 120,000 to 4,932, a 96% drop. — [新浪财经](https://finance.sina.com.cn/wm/2026-05-19/doc-inhymumi7464423.shtml)
- **Policy stance:** academic-subject tutoring stays tightly controlled, while non-academic, vocational and AI education are encouraged. Academic tutoring has not legally rebounded. — [虎嗅 双减五周年](https://www.huxiu.com/article/4880312.html); [虎嗅](https://www.huxiu.com/article/4899034.html)
- **New Oriental:** FY2026 revenue US$5.661bn, up 15.5%, with net profit up 27.8%. Growth came from study abroad, adult education, livestream e-commerce and tourism.
- **TAL (好未来):** full-year revenue US$3.009bn, up 33.7%, driven by 素养 (non-academic) courses and learning hardware.
- **Market value:** New Oriental's revenue and profit now exceed pre-双减 levels, but its market value has recovered to less than 30% of the pre-双减 level. Another article describes the market value as "只剩十分之一" (only one-tenth left). Fiscal years differ (New Oriental ends 31 May, TAL ends 28/29 Feb), so check the company filings.
- Sources for the three items above — [虎嗅](https://www.huxiu.com/article/4879624.html); [界面](https://www.jiemian.com/article/14889904.html); [腾讯新闻](https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20260203A031QO00)
- **学习机 market size:**
  - 2024 (智研咨询): 5.923 million units, up 25.5%; sales ¥19.06bn, up 37.6%.
  - Q1 2025 (洛图): 1.265 million units, up 29.4%; ¥4.02bn, up 15.8%.
  - Q3 2025 (奥维睿沃): online sales up 48.3%.
  - 2026 forecast (洛图): 6.663 million units, up only 5.4%. This implies roughly 6.3 million units in 2025, a back-calculation not reported directly.
  — [澎湃](https://www.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_31073576); [36氪](https://www.36kr.com/p/3398087911491977); [36氪](https://www.36kr.com/p/3458913811355013); [钛媒体](https://www.tmtpost.com/7639982.html)
- **Price and brands:** the ¥2,000–2,999 band was 45.9% of online unit sales in 2025, up 30.7 points year on year (national consumer-goods subsidies are a factor). The top six brands (作业帮, 学而思, 科大讯飞, 步步高, 小猿, 小度) hold 74.4% of the market. 作业帮 leads online with 32.6% of units. — same sources as above
- **Effectiveness and consumer issues:**
  - No consumer-association study and no independent controlled study of learning effects was found.
  - A 赛迪 report found 45% of users use only basic photo-search, and use of advanced AI features is under 30%.
  - Complaints include AI grading that marks correct answers wrong (学而思 flagship), the "真人随时问" (ask a real tutor anytime) feature on an ¥8,000+ device being replaced by "AI随时问", devices becoming "电子砖头" (electronic bricks) within one to two years, and bans on third-party apps.
  — [东方财富](https://finance.eastmoney.com/news/1348,202510193538029657.html); [澎湃](https://www.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_32632515); [新华网](https://www.news.cn/fortune/20250828/1eb58577e3ba4c5babc369aa70c91340/c.html); [黑猫投诉](https://tousu.sina.com.cn/articles/view/413527)

### Inferences
- 学习机 have partly become a legal channel for tutoring-style content after 双减. Their heavy use for photo-search (拍照搜题) can encourage the same answer-copying ("代劳") that the MOE guideline bans.
- Market data comes from industry trackers. Usage and effect data is weak, so the book should not claim these devices work, or that they don't, beyond what this evidence supports.

### Gaps
- No rigorous Chinese RCT or official evaluation of 学习机 learning outcomes was found.
- No sourced data was retrieved on underground academic tutoring prevalence or on household tutoring spending in 2025–26.
- No 2026 central-government 双减 revision document was found. The policy appears unchanged.

## 3. Higher education: AI majors, enrollment, graduate jobs, youth unemployment, vocational reform

### Takeaway
Universities and vocational colleges are adding AI and embodied-intelligence majors quickly. Meanwhile, a record 12.7 million graduates entered a labour market where youth unemployment (ages 16–24, excluding students) peaked at 18.9% in August 2026 and fell to 17.2% in October. The 2026 MyCOS "green card" majors shifted toward advanced manufacturing and energy, and computer-science majors "cooled".

### Cited Findings
- **Undergraduate catalogue:** MOE issued 《普通高等学校本科专业目录（2026年）》 (教高函〔2026〕2号, dated 2026-04-07).
  - It adds 38 new undergraduate majors, including "商业人工智能" (business AI) and, for the first time, 具身智能 (embodied intelligence).
  - Nine universities, including 哈工大 and 北航, were approved to offer 具身智能.
  - The new 交叉学科 (interdisciplinary) category includes 具身智能 and 脑机科学与技术 (brain–computer science).
  - Approved programmes could recruit in the 2026 gaokao.
  — [gov.cn](https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202604/content_7067247.htm); [澎湃](https://www.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_33077104); [光明网](https://m.gmw.cn/2026-04/28/content_1304436781.htm)
- **University applications:** MyCOS tallied 111 universities by 2026-08-20. They had more than 630 undergraduate majors proposed or pre-filed, and 人工智能教育 (AI education), 具身智能 and 机器人工程 (robotics engineering) were among the top ten. These are university-level applications, not final approvals. — [第一财经](https://www.yicai.com/news/103331275.html)
- **Vocational reform 1:** MOE issued 《关于深化职业教育教学关键要素改革的意见》 (教职成〔2026〕1号, dated 2026-02-04).
  - It creates a fast-track for new majors in low-altitude economy, AI and similar fields.
  - It uses big data and AI to forecast talent demand and to close majors with low employment.
  - It strengthens general AI courses.
  - Targets: 2027 (standards system) and 2035.
  — [gov.cn](https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202602/content_7057852.htm); [中新网](https://www.chinanews.com/edu/2026/02-12/10571092.shtml)
- **Vocational reform 2 [ANNOUNCED; enrollment from 2027]:** the 2026 vocational catalogue adds 27 majors (9 高职专科 higher-vocational diploma, 18 职业本科 vocational bachelor's). They include 具身智能计算技术 (embodied intelligence computing), 人形机器人工程技术 (humanoid robotics), 商业人工智能应用 (business AI applications) and 智能体通信技术 (AI agent communications). — [新华网 2026-07-16](https://www.news.cn/politics/20260716/8e1eb894423d49e2ba4f370210b0576c/c.html); [中新网 2026-08-30](https://www.chinanews.com/edu/2026/08-30/10686768.shtml)
- **Graduate numbers:** the 2026 graduating class is 12.7 million, up 480,000 (MOE, 2025-11-20). The series: 10.76m (2022), 11.58m (2023), 11.79m (2024), 12.22m (2025). — [新华网](https://www.news.cn/20251120/ead0f25dff2948dfa7f01fa78f207882/c.html); [人民网](http://edu.people.com.cn/n1/2025/1121/c367001-40608931.html); historical series from [网易](https://www.163.com/dy/article/LB123APH0556BO0T.html) (secondary)
- **Youth unemployment (NBS, ages 16–24 excluding students):**
  - August 2026: 18.9%, the highest since August 2025.
  - September 2026: 18.0%.
  - October 2026: 17.2% (released 2026-11-18).
  - October 2026, other measures: ages 25–29 at 7.3%, ages 30–59 at 3.9%, overall urban rate 5.1%.
  - NBS attributes the decline to graduates finding jobs.
  — [澎湃](https://www.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_34503131); [腾讯新闻](https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20261118A0A67700); [新浪](https://www.sina.cn/news/detail/5356921525371260.html); [NBS methodology note](https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/zxfb/202401/t20240117_1946641.html)
- **MyCOS 2026 report (《2026年中国大学生就业报告》, 2026-06-11):**
  - Undergraduate green-card majors: 电气工程及其自动化 (electrical engineering), 微电子 (microelectronics), 自动化 (automation, first time on the list), 能源与动力 (energy and power), 车辆工程 (vehicle engineering), 新能源 (new energy). All are engineering.
  - From 2022 to 2026 the green list shifted from IT toward advanced manufacturing, energy and key technologies. Media summarised the change as "计算机类集体冷却" (computer-science majors cooling as a group).
  - 2025 undergraduate red-card majors: 公共事业管理 (public administration), 音乐表演 (music performance), 绘画 (painting), 法学 (law), 美术学 (fine arts).
  — [新浪财经](https://finance.sina.cn/2026-06-11/detail-iniazpqt0773266.d.html?vt=4); [21世纪经济报道](https://m.sfccn.com/2026/6-12/yNMDE0MDVfMjE1OTQyNg.html); [东方财富](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202606113768352986.html)

### Inferences
- "AI major" ≠ guaranteed job. MyCOS's cooling of computing majors combined with record graduate supply suggests applied engineering (automation, electrical, energy) currently has better employment signals than generic CS or AI labels.
- The fast addition of vocational AI majors (enrolling from 2027) makes 职业本科 (vocational bachelor's) a more credible pathway than before. Parents should weigh it on job data, not stigma.

### Gaps
- No sourced figure for total AI-major enrollment, or for the number of universities offering 人工智能 majors in 2026.
- No MyCOS data on employment rates or salaries specifically for 人工智能 graduates.
- No data on 职业本科 enrollment scale or expansion targets.

## 4. International comparisons (UNESCO, OECD PISA 2025, US, UK, Singapore, Korea)

### Takeaway
Internationally, there are two camps. One is cautious, age-staged and supervised (UNESCO, Singapore, England's DfE, China's own guideline). The other is a fast push that has met backlash, most clearly Korea's AI textbooks, which were downgraded within a semester. PISA 2025 (September 2026) shows sharp declines in reading and maths across the OECD, while 46% of students use AI chatbots weekly to learn.

### Cited Findings
- **UNESCO, Guidance for Generative AI in Education and Research (September 2023):** governments are urged to protect data privacy and to consider a minimum age of 13 for classroom use. — [UN News](https://news.un.org/en/story/2023/09/1140477); [EU School Education Gateway](https://school-education.ec.europa.eu/en/insights/publications/guidance-generative-ai-education-and-research)
- **UNESCO AI competency framework for students:** described as the first global framework of its kind. It envisions students as "AI co-creators and responsible citizens" and stresses critical judgement of AI, foundational AI knowledge and ethical design. A companion teacher framework exists. — [UNESCO framework PDF via MIT RAISE](https://raise.mit.edu/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/391105eng.pdf)
- **OECD PISA 2025 Results, Volume I "Future-Ready Students" (8 September 2026):**
  - Reading and maths across the OECD fell to the lowest averages PISA has recorded.
  - About two-thirds of students reached Level 3 or above in computational problem solving (part of Learning in the Digital World), and about a quarter reached Levels 5–6.
  - 46% of students in OECD countries use AI chatbots weekly or more to help them learn.
  - Full LDW analysis is due May 2027, and self-regulated learning results (Volume III) also in May 2027.
  — [OECD press release](https://www.oecd.org/en/about/news/press-releases/2026/09/pisa-2025-students-reading-and-mathematics-performance-declined-sharply-across-the-oecd.html); [OECD Volume I](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/2026/09/pisa-2025-results-volume-i_5265bfb1.html); [OECD LDW page](https://www.oecd.org/en/topics/sub-issues/learning-in-the-digital-world/pisa-2025-learning-in-the-digital-world.html)
- **USA:**
  - Executive Order 14277, "Advancing Artificial Intelligence Education for American Youth", signed 2025-04-23.
  - It created a White House AI Education Task Force and a Presidential AI Challenge. One tracker reports a 2026-07-22 implementation deadline for the challenge.
  - Department of Education grant guidance on AI was issued 2025-07-22, and an AI priority for discretionary grants was finalized 2026-04-13.
  - Implementation is described as uneven.
  — [CSET Georgetown](https://cset.georgetown.edu/article/promises-and-progress/); [regulations.ai](https://regulations.ai/news/us-agencies-presidential-ai-challenge-2026-deadline); [Montreal AI Ethics](https://montrealethics.ai/ai-policy-corner-u-s-executive-order-on-advancing-ai-education-for-american-youth/)
- **UK (England):**
  - The DfE's "Generative AI in education" guidance was first published in March 2023 and last updated in August 2025. It is advisory, not statutory.
  - The DfE sees "more immediate benefits and fewer risks from teacher-facing use".
  - Schools must check with their data protection officer before entering pupil data into AI tools.
  - Training modules were published June 2025, and product safety standards were updated January 2026 (secondary source).
  — [Structural Learning summary](https://www.structural-learning.com/post/dfe-guidance-on-ai-in-schools); [TES](https://tes.com/magazine/news/general/ai-guidance-for-schools-from-dfe)
- **Singapore:**
  - MOE frames its approach as the "Four Learns": learn about AI, learn to use AI, learn with AI, and "most importantly, learn beyond AI" (2026 Committee of Supply).
  - AI is introduced gradually from Primary 4 under teacher supervision. Secondary students progressively use AI independently on personal learning devices.
  - Students use MOE-built tools in the Student Learning Space, not general-purpose chatbots. Their data is anonymised and not used to train external models.
  — [MOE forum reply, 2026-04-13](https://www.moe.gov.sg/news/forum-letter-replies/20260413-schools-introduce-ai-in-approach-that-is-age-and-development-appropriate); [MOE parliamentary reply, 2026-05-06](https://www.moe.gov.sg/news/parliamentary-replies/20260506-ai-usage-in-schools)
- **South Korea (rollout and rollback):**
  - AI digital textbooks (AIDT) launched in March 2025. Adoption was about 30%.
  - A January 2025 bill to downgrade them was vetoed.
  - On 2025-08-04 the National Assembly passed an amendment to the Elementary and Secondary Education Act that reclassified them as "educational materials", effective on promulgation. Schools can now choose whether to use them, and the government funding basis was removed.
  - Use was projected to fall to 2–3% in the second semester, and publishers protested.
  — [Asia Economy](https://www.asiae.co.kr/en/article/2025080418420589912); [Asia Economy, 2–3%](https://www.asiae.co.kr/en/article/2025080509423174452); [Korea Herald via Inquirer/ANN](https://globalnation.inquirer.net/287225/south-korea-pulls-plug-on-ai-textbooks)

### Inferences
- China's staging (no independent open-ended use in primary) sits between UNESCO's age-13 suggestion and Singapore's supervised use from Primary 4. The international consensus favours supervised, purpose-built tools for younger children.
- Korea is a useful cautionary tale for parents. A top-down AI textbook pushed out quickly, with weak teacher readiness and cost concerns, was reversed within a semester. "More AI in school" is not automatically better.
- PISA 2025 shows foundational reading and maths falling while chatbot use is widespread. This supports a family strategy that protects core literacy and numeracy.

### Gaps
- China did not participate as a whole in PISA 2022 and 2025. Mainland participation in PISA 2025 was not verified, so there is no direct comparison.
- The release date of the UNESCO student competency framework (believed to be September 2024) was not confirmed.
- No status was found for Korea's AIDT after the autumn 2025 semester (adoption in 2026).
- UK content of the national curriculum review on AI was not retrieved.

## 5. Implications for parents: what schools will and won't cover, what families should add

### Takeaway
Chinese schools will provide basic AI literacy: a few hours a year, staged by age, focused on concepts, ethics and supervised use. Exams will keep rewarding unaided mastery. Schools will not provide deep, individualized AI fluency, a healthy home-use regime or career-path judgement. Those fall to families.

### Cited Findings
- Schools are required to provide general AI education (Beijing at least 8 hours a year; national "开齐开足开好"), integrated into other subjects and staged by age. — [新京报](https://www.bjnews.com.cn/detail/1741356871129813.html); [emic](https://www.emic.edu.cn/site/webfile/zcwj/2026-04-10/7755.html)
- Primary pupils should use open-ended generative AI only with teacher or parent help. This explicitly gives parents a role. — [澎湃](https://www.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_30802716)
- High-stakes exams block AI help (gaokao photo-recognition shutdowns). — [IT之家](https://www.ithome.com/0/859/550.htm); [36氪](https://www.36kr.com/p/3825861613687426)
- Learning devices are mostly used for photo-search, and their AI grading has known errors. — [东方财富](https://finance.eastmoney.com/news/1348,202510193538029657.html)
- Graduate job signals favour applied engineering, and generic CS has cooled. — [新浪财经/MyCOS](https://finance.sina.cn/2026-06-11/detail-iniazpqt0773266.d.html?vt=4)
- Singapore's "learn beyond AI" framing (human qualities beyond tool use) is a policy-level endorsement of non-AI competencies. — [MOE](https://www.moe.gov.sg/news/forum-letter-replies/20260413-schools-introduce-ai-in-approach-that-is-age-and-development-appropriate)

### Inferences
- **Families should add:**
  - Supervised home AI use for primary-age children, matching the MOE rule.
  - Habits of verifying and cross-checking AI output, which the guideline expects from junior secondary.
  - Firm "no 代劳" rules for homework.
  - Protected time for reading, writing and maths without AI, given exam design and PISA declines.
  - Project-based making and inquiry.
  - Career exploration that uses employment data such as MyCOS lists and vocational options, rather than "AI major" labels.
- **When buying a 学习机, parents should:**
  - Judge it on how it is used, since photo-search can undermine learning.
  - Check AI grading accuracy.
  - Check the device's lifespan and whether it can run third-party apps.
- Rules vary by province. Parents should check their own city's AI education plan and whether AI course results enter 综合素质评价.

### Gaps
- No Chinese empirical study was found on how school AI classes affect student outcomes.
- No official data was found on how many schools actually meet the required hours. The gap between plan and reality is unknown.
